Krypt Studio's **code** is MIT-licensed: you can use it, change it, fork it and sell what you build with it. The MIT licence does **not** grant any rights to the Krypt name or logo. This policy explains what that means in practice.

## The marks

"Krypt", "Krypt Studio", the Krypt logo and the Krypt wordmark (together, "the Krypt marks") identify software made and released by the Krypt team (https://krypt.cc). They are not licensed under the MIT licence.

## Forks must rebrand

If you distribute a modified version of Krypt Studio, or a build you made yourself from changed source:

- **Give it a different name** that isn't confusingly similar to "Krypt" or "Krypt Studio". "Krypt Studio Plus", "KryptStudio Pro" and "K-Studio" are all too close.
- **Replace the logo and icons** (`resources/krypt.png`, `resources/krypt.ico`, `build/icon.png`, `public/krypt.png`) with your own.
- **Change the identifiers that point at us:** the app id (`cc.krypt.studio`), the Discord Rich Presence application, the krypt.cc links and the Discord invite. Your users' support requests and presence shouldn't land with us.
- **Don't imply endorsement.** Don't say or suggest that your fork is made, approved or supported by Krypt.

You **may**:

- Say truthfully that your project is "based on Krypt Studio" or "a fork of Krypt Studio", in plain text, as long as the name of your product is your own.
- Keep the copyright notices and `LICENSE` file, which the MIT licence requires anyway.
- Use the Krypt marks to refer to the original project, for example in a comparison or a tutorial.
- Distribute **unmodified** official builds, and build the unmodified source for your own use, with the marks intact.

## Unofficial packages

Packaging an unmodified release for a package manager (winget, Scoop, Chocolatey) is welcome. Please keep the name "Krypt Studio", link back to this repository, and say clearly that the package is community-maintained if it isn't published by us.
